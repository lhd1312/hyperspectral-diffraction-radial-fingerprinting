# Interpretation and Reproduction Boundaries

- **Physics terminology:** the generator uses intensity cubes and Fresnel-inspired radial wavelength scaling, spectral/spatial smoothness, intensity range/energy, and class-reference priors. There is no recovered complex phase, complete optical propagation solver, or full PDE-residual PINN. Physics regularization does not establish that every generated cube is physically realizable.
- **Data dependence:** class-specific historical spectral alignment is retained for reproducing the ROI experiment only. Unknown-class edge inference requires the separate class-independent calibration/classifier. Document whether calibration parameters were estimated before or after splitting.
- **Independence:** ROI test isolation is enforced by directory separation, but slide/preparation/field independence must be established from metadata. Per-ROI bootstrap does not automatically provide preparation-level uncertainty. The internal CNN validation and post-hoc test selection caveats are in the reproduction guide.
- **Synthetic data:** generated cubes are training augmentation, never additional independent experimental replicates. Mean synthetic-vs-real differences are not paired reconstruction errors unless inputs are truly paired. Old-weight zero-shot square-domain transfer previews are not quantitative evidence for a retrained square-ROI generator.
- **Detection:** fixed boxes and one keypoint encode center localization, not object outlines. State the confidence threshold, center-distance rule, matching implementation, crop eligibility, and field-level split. The RKNN evaluation preserves a finite-penalty radius-aware assignment, not a guaranteed lexicographic maximum-cardinality matcher. Do not mix it with other matching rules without re-evaluating all comparators.
- **Full-field classes:** the initial independent full-sequence classification evaluation did not have a DQB field in every analysis. Later DQB manual count checks do not automatically establish a five-class full-field classification confusion matrix.
- **Quantification:** the empirical count-concentration fit is for 10 micrometre PVC and the recorded loading geometry, volume, dilution, field area, and concentration range. Applying it to spores without material-specific recovery/calibration gives a PVC-equivalent estimate, not validated absolute pathogen concentration. Unknown wet area cannot be replaced by the coverslip area without validation. No detection limit is established without an appropriate blank/low-concentration design.
- **Timing:** sequence analysis excludes stage scanning and camera acquisition. Specify warm-up, repetitions, sample standard deviation, file-cache state, image/ROI counts, model initialization, rendering and file output, CPU threads and device clocks. The reference Jetson workflow and optimized RK workflow are not automatically an equal-work timing comparison. No new benchmark is produced by the packaging tests.
- **Hardware:** camera firmware and stage controller compatibility must be verified separately. Missing CH341 kernel support or device permissions are operating-system issues, not fixed by the inference model. Do not execute motion commands until travel limits and orientation are checked.
- **Availability:** source-only release is not complete experimental reproducibility. Real measurements, splits/labels, pretrained artifacts, model exports, and source data for figures need versioned public records or an explicitly justified access route. Their public URLs are not yet supplied here.
